# Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 15, 2026

LegalEase values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data when you use our application.

## Data Collection & Usage

- **On-Device Processing:** All user data, files, and chat history are processed and stored locally on your device whenever possible.
- **AI API Usage:** When you use AI features (e.g., chat, document analysis), your input and relevant data are securely sent to third-party AI APIs (such as Google Gemini) for processing. Only the minimum necessary data is transmitted.
- **No Unnecessary Sharing:** We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties except as required to provide AI functionality.

## Data Security

- All data stored on your device is kept private and is not uploaded to our servers.
- API requests are transmitted securely using industry-standard encryption.

## User Control

- You may delete your data and files from the app at any time.
- We do not retain copies of your data after processing unless you explicitly save or export it.

## Third-Party Services

- Use of AI APIs is subject to the privacy policies of those providers (e.g., Google Gemini).

## Changes to This Policy

- We may update this Privacy Policy as needed. Changes will be posted in the app and/or project repository.

For questions, contact the maintainers or see the project documentation.
